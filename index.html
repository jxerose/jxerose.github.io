Systems · macOS

Controller drivers

Two game controllers that macOS refused to see. I traced each problem down to the USB and HID layer and wrote small Swift tools that fix them — no kernel extensions, no special permissions.

switch2wake
A Switch 2 Pro Controller sends nothing until it gets a vendor handshake on a USB interface macOS ignores. This claims that interface and sends it.
padbridge
Reads a pad Apple's GameController framework won't list, straight from IOKit HID, and streams it into the game over UDP.

How it works

From problem
to hand-off

The same way SiteMedic was built: screens first, then working software in small steps.

  1. Talk it through

    You tell me the problem in your words and what "done" looks like.

  2. Click the mockup

    You click through the screens before anything is built, and we fix the layout while it's cheap.

  3. Build in steps

    Each step works on its own and you can try it. Nothing disappears into a black box for a month.

  4. Hand it over

    You own the code, the hosting and the logins. I'm around for fixes after launch.
